Smart Mess · Problem

The main-dish line gets long before anyone reacts

In an IIT Guwahati hostel mess, the main-dish counter (dosa, paratha, roti) is the bottleneck. Staff only see the line once it is long; students cannot tell when to come.

Users

  • Mess manager / counter staff: how fast to serve in the next 15 minutes
  • Students: go now, or come back in 15 minutes

Our question

From door counts and 5-minute counter counts, how long will the line be in 5, 10 and 15 minutes, how long is the wait, and what service rate keeps it under 5 minutes?

Mean line length by meal and weekday. Weekday rushes: breakfast 8:30-9:00, lunch 12:45-1:00 (Wed-Fri), dinner after 9:00; weekend breakfast burst at 9:45.

Workflow · Collection · Solution

Three phones, two counts, one forecast

1 DoorIN / OUT tap per person, timestamped
→
2 Counter, every 5 minpeople in line + main-dish units made (= service rate)
→
3 Stopwatch~10 people/meal: join to served
→
4 Validategaps, IN/OUT drift, open Parquet
→
5 Forecastline in 5/10/15 min + 80% band
→
6 Decidewait = line / service rate, then alert + advice

Collection

  • Offline phone tap-logger exports the exact schema
  • Blank means missed. We never guess
  • No cameras, names or roll numbers. Manager approval before any counting
  • Units per plate converts "made per minute" into people per minute

Physics first

  • Line(t) = line(t-1) + people in - people served
  • Capacity measured only while a line exists (otherwise "made" = demand)
  • Wait = line / service rate, checked by stopwatch
  • Our first formula, (in - made)/3, kept as a baseline: it tracks growth, not the line

Model

  • Predicts the change in line length 5/10/15 min ahead
  • Inputs: line now, recent arrivals, service rate, dish speed, same-weekday arrival profile
  • XGBoost vs small neural net vs 4 baselines; XGB chosen
  • Tested on days it never saw, incl. each weekday predicted from the weeks before
Data: CSV + Parquet with embedded schema · rebuild: python run_all.py (REPRODUCE.md) Observed / inferred tagged on every column

What changes

  • Manager: acts 10-15 minutes earlier with a target service rate, instead of reacting to a long line
  • Students: "go now" or "come in 15 min"
  • After each meal: peak, minutes over target, the service rate that was needed

Data Collection Challenges

  • Tried accessing camera recordings but failed due to privacy issues and cameras not working.
  • To resolve counting issues, we built an app to record: people in (entering mess and picking thali), people out (completed meal and returned thali), and serving rate (people leaving the queue).
